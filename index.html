Bernie Calderon Magtalas is an infrastructure economist and licensed electrical engineer. He is a Supervising Economic Development Specialist in the Infrastructure Staff of the Department of Economy, Planning, and Development (DEPDev) Investment Programming Group, where he leads project appraisal, planning, and policy formulation across the energy, transportation, and wider public infrastructure sectors.

He began his career at the Department of Energy (DOE) in December 2015, supporting renewable energy and rural electrification programs in remote, off-grid, and conflict-affected areas, and joined the National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA, now DEPDev) in September 2016.

Methods and tools

These methods are applied under the NEDA (now DEPDev) Reference Manual on Project Development and Evaluation and the Investment Coordination Committee (ICC) guidelines.

Appraisal framework
Integrated project analysis across market, technical, financial, economic, institutional, social, and risk modules; incremental with-and-without-project comparison; least-cost alternatives across technology, location, scale, and timing
Economic analysis
Shadow pricing with ICC parameters (10% social discount rate, shadow exchange and wage rates); conversion factors, removal of transfers, valuation of externalities; EIRR, ENPV, and benefit-cost ratio
Financial analysis
Real and nominal cash-flow models from the owner, total-investment, and government budget viewpoints; FIRR against the weighted average cost of capital; annual liquidity and debt-service checks
Risk and sensitivity
ICC sensitivity cases (costs +10% and +20%, benefits −10% and −20%, combined), switching values; PPP risk identification, allocation, and mitigation costing
Social and distributional
Stakeholder analysis reconciling financial and economic results; social impacts and willingness to pay
Climate and environment
Greenhouse gas abatement costing, climate resilience, nature-related impacts
Investment programming
Multi-criteria analysis to prioritize projects in the national infrastructure investment pipeline
Policy analysis
Comparative analysis of legislation and regulatory frameworks, such as benchmarking the natural gas industry bill against the electric power industry’s market framework
Statistics
Confirmatory factor analysis, structural equation modeling
Analysis
R, Python, Excel (economic and financial models)
Visualization
Interactive web dashboards built from R and Python outputs
Energy modeling
