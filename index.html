The evaluation challenge

As AIIB expands across sovereign-backed and nonsovereign-backed operations, CEIU needs independent evaluations that explain what shaped results, translate findings into lessons, and inform improvements in future operations.

Explain what shaped results

Examine how project design, appraisal quality, implementation readiness, institutional context, and risk allocation influenced infrastructure outcomes.

Translate findings into lessons

Turn evaluation findings into lessons for Early Learning Assessments (ELAs), Project Learning Reviews (PLRs), thematic evaluations, and knowledge products.

Inform future operations

Apply evaluation lessons to strengthen project design, delivery, risk allocation, and performance.
